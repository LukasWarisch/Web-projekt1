History and Origins of Football

From Ancient Games to the Modern Era

Football as we know it today originated in 19th-century England. However, ball games similar to football were played thousands of years ago in China, Greece, and Rome.

Key Historical Milestones

  • 1863: Establishment of The Football Association (FA) in England and the creation of the first official rules.
  • 1872: The first international match, played between England and Scotland.
  • 1904: Formation of FIFA, the international governing body, in Paris.
  • 1930: The first FIFA World Cup tournament held in Uruguay.

Most Famous Eras of Football

  • The Golden Era of Pelé and Brazil (1950s–1970s)
  • The Total Football of the Netherlands (1970s)
  • The Era of Diego Maradona (1980s)
  • The Modern Rivalry: Messi vs. Ronaldo (21st century)
